Change hidden settings in your car

          
Some cars have settings you can switch on or off, like lights that stay on after you lock the car. This tab reads those settings and lets you change them.

          
    
            
  • It only changes saved settings. It never touches the software inside the car's computers.
    • 
            
  • The old setting is saved before every change, so you can always put it back from Backups.
    • 
            
  • Nothing is written until you press Write to car and confirm.
    • 
            
  • Engine off, ignition on, battery at 12.4 V or more. A battery charger is best. Don't unplug the cable while it works.
